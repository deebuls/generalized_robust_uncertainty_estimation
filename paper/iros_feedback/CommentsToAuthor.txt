Comments to author (Associate Editor)
=====================================


Overall, the paper is promising and the topic is certainly
of interest to the community. However, the paper has severe
weak points in the evaluation making it not sufficiently
feasible to asses and clearly see the contribution. Also
the magnitude of contribution/improvement over the state of
the art is not clear enough from the manuscript, especially
due to a lacking related work section and evaluation and
the choice of baselines. The authors should take the
recommendations of the reviewers seriously to make this a
stronger paper.
